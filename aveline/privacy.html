Your practice. Your privacy.

Aveline · Eric Stewart · Updated October 25, 2026

Aveline is a general adult fitness app developed by Eric Stewart. No Aveline account is required.

Your training records

Your optional first name, equipment preferences, saved programs, workout history, repetitions, weights, effort feedback, and unfinished sessions are stored locally on your device. Aveline does not upload these training records to a server. They are not sent to RevenueCat. Your device backup settings may include app data.

Optional Hush connection and daily check-ins

If you choose to import a Hush connection file, Aveline first lets you review and confirm the import. The snapshot contains dated energy, discomfort and fatigue check-ins, optional period start dates, and settings indicating whether cycle patterns and general adult training are supported. Notes, medications and fertility records are excluded. This is a manual snapshot, not live synchronization. Aveline stores its imported copy and any daily check-in you enter locally on your device. These records are not uploaded to a server, included in purchase requests or used for advertising.

Open the Hush connection controls in Today or You to disconnect and remove the imported copy. Delete my data removes the imported copy and daily check-in as well as your training records. Your training backup does not include this separate health context. Deleting Aveline data does not remove Hush’s original journal, exported files, device backups or purchase records.

Memberships

Apple processes membership payments and RevenueCat checks purchases and subscription access. RevenueCat receives an automatically generated app user identifier, store transaction and receipt information, subscription status, and app/device technical information needed to operate that service. Aveline does not provide your name, training records, or advertising identifier to RevenueCat, and does not receive your payment card details.

Purchase history is used to provide subscription features, validate transactions, prevent fraud, and understand subscription performance. Aveline uses RevenueCat’s anonymous app identifiers and does not connect them to your optional first name or training records.

No advertising tracking

Aveline has no advertising SDK, does not sell training data, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It does not request HealthKit, location, camera, microphone, or contacts access.

Your controls

Open You to export a backup, import one, or delete local records. Sharing an export sends its contents to the destination you choose. Deleting local records does not delete exported copies, device backups, or Apple’s purchase records, and does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions in your Apple account.

Optional crash reports

Aveline uses PLCrashReporter to keep a technical report on your device after an unexpected native crash. It records information such as the app version, operating system, and stack traces. We do not add your training journal to these reports. There is no automatic upload. The latest report is held in the app cache, which the operating system may clear. If you choose Share crash report, Apple’s share sheet lets you review the file and select its destination. Reports sent to support are used to investigate the problem.

Support and retention

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. Avoid sending health details or complete workout backups unless necessary. Local records remain until you remove them or the app’s data. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Contact us for access or deletion requests relating to information we hold; purchase records may be retained where required by law or the payment provider.

External services

Opening a technique reference or policy website connects to that website under its own policies. These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, whose server may process standard request information such as IP addresses.
