A little help, when you need it.

Aveline · Eric Stewart · Updated September 9, 2026

Aveline makes room for a consistent movement practice with strength, Pilates-inspired movement, and recovery.

Contact support

Email EricLStewart@pm.me. Include your app version, device, and a short description of the issue. Do not send payment card details or sensitive health information.

Getting started

Choose your available equipment, then explore a program in Studio. Fit this to my day adjusts the session to your time, experience, and energy. Review preparation and movement cues before beginning.

Resume a workout

Reopen Aveline on the same device to resume an unfinished session. Completed sets and rest state are saved as you go. Leaving and discarding a session does not add a completed workout to history.

Restore purchases

When paid memberships launch, open You, then the membership plan and Restore purchases using the Apple account that made the purchase. If the store cannot connect, check your connection and try again. Free sessions remain available.

Back up or delete records

Open You to export, import, or delete your data. Import replaces existing records after confirmation. Records do not automatically sync between devices. Keep your export somewhere private.

Membership management

Manage or cancel with Apple. Deleting local data does not cancel a subscription. This app is currently in private preview; it is not yet available for purchase.
