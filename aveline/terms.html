General fitness

Aveline provides general adult exercise routines and record keeping. It does not provide medical advice, assess your form, or diagnose or treat conditions. Its routines are not designed for pregnancy, postpartum recovery, or injury rehabilitation. Seek individual professional guidance where appropriate. Stop exercise for pain, dizziness, or unusual shortness of breath. Results vary; no physical outcome is guaranteed.

Memberships

The app is in a private, no-charge preview until its paid launch. When available, Studio is an optional auto-renewing Apple subscription. The app displays the store price and billing period before confirmation. Apple charges your account on confirmation and renews the subscription unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel through Apple subscriptions. Deleting the app or local data does not cancel your subscription. Refund requests are handled through Apple, subject to applicable rights.

Your records

Records are stored on your device and do not automatically sync between devices. Keep exported backups if you need a separate copy. Importing replaces local records after confirmation. Session minutes include rest while the app is visible and do not represent active exercise or calories.
