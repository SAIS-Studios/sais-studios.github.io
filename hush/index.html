Privacy-first menstrual tracking

Hush

A calm, private cycle tracker. Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

About

Hush helps you track your menstrual cycle, predict your next period and ovulation window, log symptoms, and read phase-specific health recommendations. Every entry is stored only on your device — there's no account to create, no data sent anywhere, and no analytics watching you.

Get the app

Hush is available on the App Store. Search for "Hush" or use the App Store link from your iPhone.
