Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Optional Aveline connection

You can choose to export an Aveline connection snapshot containing up to one year of dated energy, discomfort and fatigue check-ins and, when calendar estimates are enabled, period start dates. The snapshot also indicates whether cycle patterns and general adult training are supported. It excludes notes, medications, fertility records, identity and purchase information. You review the categories and consent before exporting. The file is saved or shared through the destination you choose. Aveline requires a separate import confirmation and stores its copy locally. This is a manual snapshot, not automatic sync. Hush does not send these records to a server or include them in purchase requests. Deleting Hush data does not delete copies in Aveline, exported files or device backups. Disconnect Hush in Aveline to remove its imported copy.

Your journal

Hush stores cycle entries, symptoms, reflections, medication records, and preferences locally on your device or browser. You do not need a Hush account. The app does not send these journal records in its purchase requests.

Purchases and external links

Apple and RevenueCat process purchase and subscription information, including identifiers and transaction data. The SDK may contact its service to check entitlements when Hush opens. Their policies apply to that processing. Opening a source or support link contacts the destination service.

Exports and device backups

An exported JSON, CSV, or doctor-summary file contains personal information. Save and share it carefully. Device backups and exported files are separate copies outside Hush’s own journal. Local storage does not guarantee protection on a lost, shared, or compromised device.

Your controls

Settings includes export, import, and deletion. Deleting local records does not remove copies you exported, device backups, or store transaction records. Clearing browser or app data can remove your journal. A recovery copy of an unreadable local record may be kept on the same device to avoid silently losing it; deleting all data also removes this copy.

Data use

Hush does not include advertising trackers or send journal entries to an analytics service. Subscription processing remains separate. No cloud journal sync is offered in this build.

Contact

Contact support@cyclekit.app with privacy questions. Avoid sending sensitive journal records unless necessary for your request.
